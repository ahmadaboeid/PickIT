Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This privacy policy explains how PickIT ("the app", "we", "our") handles information. KidsTube is a parental-control app that lets a parent choose which YouTube channels a child may watch.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for a name, email address, or phone number, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

The list of channels a parent chooses is stored locally on the device only, and is never sent to us or to any server we control.

Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • YouTube Data API — used to look up channels and their public video information (titles, thumbnails, video IDs). This use is governed by the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube embedded player — videos play through YouTube's official embedded player, which may set its own cookies and is subject to YouTube's and Google's privacy policies. See Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is designed as a parental-control tool. It does not collect personal information from children or anyone else. The app's purpose is to restrict a child's viewing to channels chosen by a parent.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
